About FundiMtaani

Fundi (craftsman) + mtaani (in the neighbourhood): a directory connecting Nairobi residents directly with verified local tradespeople.

Our mission

Finding a reliable plumber, electrician or carpenter in Nairobi often means asking around on estate WhatsApp groups and hoping for the best. FundiMtaani fixes that: one searchable directory of vetted fundis, organised by trade and estate, with real neighbour reviews and transparent rates — and you contact the fundi directly, with no middleman taking a cut.

How the platform works

  1. 1

    Fundis list free

    Tradespeople register their trade, estate, rates and experience. Every phone number is verified before the profile goes live.

  2. 2

    Residents search & compare

    Filter by trade or estate. Compare ratings, read neighbour reviews and check indicative rates.

  3. 3

    Direct contact

    Call or WhatsApp the fundi yourself, agree the job and pay only when the work satisfies you.

What we stand for

  • Verified, not just listed — identity and phone checks before any profile goes live.
  • Direct dealing — residents and fundis talk to each other; nobody takes a commission.
  • Honest reviews — only people who hired a fundi shape their reputation.
  • Safety first — clear payment-safety guidance on every page.
